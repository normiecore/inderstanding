The view from above

Marcus Aurelius returns several times to an exercise of picturing human life from high up, all of it at once: crowds, farms, weddings, births, deaths, mourning, markets (Meditations 7.48 and 9.30, paraphrased). The faint points around your sky stand for other people's lives, each with figures of its own.
